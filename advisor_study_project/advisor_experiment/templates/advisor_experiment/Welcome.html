Welcome to the Pixel Prediction Study!

In this study, you will see grids with two colors of pixels: red and blue. Each image will be 51% one color and 49% the other.

Your task is to state your confidence that the majority color is Red. You will do this twice: once before consulting an advisor's opinion and once after.

The task structure will flow as follows: You will see a pixel grid, then be asked to adjust the slider (shown below) to state your initial confidence that the majority color is red.

50% chance Red

You will then get a piece of advice about the color, and be asked to re-evaluate your confidence with a second slider.

Bonus Payment

You will be paid a base of $5 plus the chance to earn an $8 bonus based on your performance. Payment will be determined by a lottery based on your confidence "r". The lottery is structured as follows:

If actual majority is…Your win probability
Redp = 1 − (1−r)²
Bluep = 1 − r²

Say you think there is an 80% chance the majority is red. r = 0.8.
If Red is the majority, the odds you will get paid p = 1 − (1−r)² = 1 − (1−0.8)² = 96% chance.
If it is not the majority, the odds you will get paid p = 1 − r² = 1 − 0.8² = 36% chance.
So, if you really believe it is 80% likely to be red, your expected payout is .8×96% + .2×36% = 84% chance of getting the bonus.

This payoff scheme means it is always in your best interest to give your best guess of the probability that Red is the majority.

You will be paid based on one initial guess and one final guess, chosen at random, from EACH block. This means there are 12 total random responses that will factor into your bonus payment, with a maximum of $8.
