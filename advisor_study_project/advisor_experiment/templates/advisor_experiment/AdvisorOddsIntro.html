Don't worry! You will have help! Every block, you will be assigned a new advisor from each of the two pixel consulting groups: Dots & Co. and PixelHouse. You can use these advisors to help you make your predictions.

Dots & Co. has a better reputation for providing accurate advisors, but neither team is perfect! Advisors from each company have different accuracy rates. The accuracy levels and the number of advisors at each level are given in the tables below.

Every block, each company will send you a SINGLE ADVISOR with a SINGLE ACCURACY. For example, Dots & Co. might send you an advisor who is 90% accurate, and PixelHouse might send you an advisor who is 75% accurate. Figuring out how accurate your assigned advisors are is important to succeed.
